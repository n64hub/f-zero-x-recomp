# The load run of the desktop build's save check (N64Recomp-browser's tools/frame_dump.mjs --saves), after
# tools/save_plan.txt, the program started again in the same folder: the game reads its SRAM as it boots, and Sound
# Mode is Mono (gSettingSoundMode, 0x80111840, 1) at the title only if it came off the save (a new SRAM says Stereo,
# 0). Nothing is written by then. Then the same route to the options sets it back to Stereo, a second save written
# through the check's links.
0 until 0x800DCE44 0xFF 0x0 90
1 until 0x80111840 0xFFFF0000 0x10000 5
1.5 shot title
2 saves same
3 until 0x800DCE44 0xFF 0x7 30 Enter
5 key S 100
5.5 until 0x800CD380 0xFFFFFFFF 0x5 10 S
6 key D 100
6.5 until 0x800CD380 0xFFFFFFFF 0x6 10 D
7.5 until 0x800DCE44 0xFF 0x14 15 Space
9.5 until 0x8011EDD8 0xFFFFFFFF 0x3 10 S
10.5 shot options_mono
11 key D 100
11.5 until 0x80111840 0xFFFF0000 0x0 5
12 shot options_stereo
12.5 saves changed 15
16 end
