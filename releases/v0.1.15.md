The title and the menus fill the window's height. Like v0.1.14 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release's first version (*F-Zero X (U)*, `CFZE`, a big-endian `.z64` of 16 MiB, SHA-1 `5f658e88ffa9de23cba6986a8fd3d3a90d7b4340`). Other regions, revisions and the 64DD's Expansion Kit are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `FZeroXRecompiled-v0.1.15-windows-x64.zip` and run `FZeroXRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `FZeroXRecompiled-v0.1.15-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `FZeroXRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- The title, the menus and the screens between them fill the window's height (or its width, in a window no wider than 4:3), their shape kept: before, they stood in a 4:3 frame with black above and below it, the machine select's backdrop was boxed, and in a very wide window the title was stretched. Races are unchanged.

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/f-zero-x-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#f-zero-x).
